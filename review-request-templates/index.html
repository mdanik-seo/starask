    How to ask without being pushy

    1. Ask soon. The same day or the next morning works best.
    2. Keep it short. Thank them, ask once, and put the link on its own.
    3. Make it personal. Use their first name and mention what you did for them.
    4. Follow up once. One reminder a few days later is fine. More than that annoys people.

    Questions

    Should I text or email review requests?

    Text messages are usually opened faster, so they work well right after a visit. Email suits longer jobs, invoices, and customers who prefer it. Always have the customer's permission to message them.

    Can I offer a discount for a review?

    No. Google's policies don't allow offering rewards or discounts in exchange for reviews. Ask every customer the same way and let them decide.

    How many reminders should I send?

    One reminder, three to five days after the first message, is plenty.
